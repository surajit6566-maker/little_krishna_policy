Privacy Policy

Little Krishna: Brain Puzzle Adventures

Effective Date: November 23, 2026 • Package ID: com.aistudio.littlekrishna.vrindv • Contact: surajit6566@gmail.com

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Little Krishna: Brain Puzzle Adventures ("the App", "we", "our") handles information when you play our Android puzzle adventure game. We are committed to protecting the privacy of our players, including children and families.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not require account registration, login, or user profiles. We do not directly collect, store, or transmit personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, phone number, photos, contacts, or precise GPS location.

3. Local Data Stored on Your Device

Your gameplay progress and preferences are stored strictly locally on your device using Android Jetpack DataStore Preferences. This local data includes:

  • Unlocked chapters and levels (Levels 1–30)
  • Earned stars and golden coin balance
  • Unlocked and equipped Bal Krishna costumes
  • Daily Challenge completion date
  • Audio settings (sound effects and background music toggles) and Free Play mode setting
Your Control: You can delete all locally saved game progress at any time from inside the App via Settings → Reset All Progress, or by uninstalling the App or clearing its storage in Android System Settings.

4. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to show rewarded video ads after level completion so players can optionally earn bonus in-game coins.

While we do not collect personal data ourselves, Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain technical and device data to deliver, measure, and prevent fraud in ads, including:

  • Advertising ID (AAID) or app-set device identifiers
  • IP address and approximate (coarse/country-level) region
  • Device model, OS version, language, and network connection status
  • Ad impression, view, and reward-completion diagnostics

In the App, AdMob requests are configured with a family-appropriate maximum ad content rating of G (General Audiences). To learn more about how Google handles data in advertising, please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

5. Permissions Used by the App

The App requests only minimal network permissions required to load and display rewarded ads:

  • android.permission.INTERNET — Connects to Google AdMob to load rewarded ads.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — Checks whether a network connection is available before requesting an ad.

No camera, microphone, storage/media, contacts, SMS, or location permissions are requested or used.

6. Children's Privacy

We design Little Krishna: Brain Puzzle Adventures to be safe for children and families. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age of consent in your region). Parents and guardians can limit ad tracking or reset the Advertising ID in their Android device's Google Settings (Settings → Google → Ads). If you believe any personal data has been inadvertently collected, please contact us and we will promptly address the issue.

7. Data Security

Because gameplay data stays locally in your device's private app sandbox and ad requests use encrypted HTTPS connections provided by the Google Mobile Ads SDK, your information is protected against unauthorized access during normal use.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time if we add new features or update third-party SDKs. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised Effective Date.
